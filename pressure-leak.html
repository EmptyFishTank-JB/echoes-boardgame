// Echo Event 01 — Risk Selection

Pressure Leak

Pick a Tier before you roll — it sets your Champion Die's ceiling and your payout. Lower Tiers pay bigger but leave almost no room for error.

Wager: Stake one resource before rolling. No resource = automatic failure.
Win: Champion Die beats or matches Opposing Die, and stays at or under your Tier's ceiling, on any of 3 rolls.
Fail: 3 rolls exhausted without meeting your Tier — wager lost, Noise penalty scales with Tier chosen.
Commitment: Your Tier locks in the moment you roll. No changing your mind mid-attempt.
