Core Stabilization

The gravity tether is out of alignment, violently swinging the ship's center of mass. Ride out three pulses of correction and hope the tether settles near true neutral before the stress tears the bulkhead apart.

Wager: Stake one resource before rolling. No resource = automatic failure.
Each Pulse: Roll both dice. The net (Blue − Red) applies to the running total automatically — no choice, just the dice.
Perfect Balance (0): Wager tripled. Glancing Save (±1): Wager doubled. Steadied (±2): Wager returned, no bonus.
Leveled Out (±3): Wager lost, no Noise. Hull Fracture (±4 or beyond): Wager lost, +1 Noise per point away from zero.
