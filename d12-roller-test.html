5x D12 Skill Check

Up to 3 rolls. Pick the faces this check needs, then roll — click a die between rolls to keep it; the Roll button only re-rolls whatever's still unlocked.

Void: kills that die for the rest of the check — locked out automatically, can't be re-rolled or kept.
Wild: counts as Tech, DNA, Tools, or either Hazard face.
Noise / Noise Spike: +1 / +2 Noise each, only from the final roll of the check — reroll them away on a later roll and they don't count.
Required Faces (click to cycle — locks in once you roll)
