Echo Events

Certain cards in the Echo Deck are marked as Echo Events. When drawn, an Echo Event interrupts the current action — a room discovery, a terminal interaction — and must be fully resolved before play continues. The mini-games below are the Echo Events. Each one is a small dice-driven gamble that plays out in the middle of a turn, with real resources on the line.

Echo Events cost no additional actions to resolve, but they cannot be ignored. Every Echo Event requires the active player to stake a wager before any dice are rolled.

Universal Wager Rules

  • Mandatory: Every Echo Event requires staking one resource before any dice are rolled. Scrap, Salvage, Components, or Data — any resource may be wagered unless the mini-game says otherwise.
  • No Resources: Automatic failure. +4 Noise immediately. No rolls taken. Continue to the next card draw.
  • Donation: A crewmate may cover the wager if physically present in the same room when the event triggers.
  • Win: The wagered resource is returned. Some mini-games award an additional resource of the same type on top.
  • Fail: The resource is permanently lost. A Noise penalty is applied, specific to each mini-game.

Pressure Leak

Risk Selection

2× D6 — Champion (Blue) & Opposing (Red) 3 Rolls

The hull groans. A seal somewhere in this section has given way and atmosphere is bleeding into the void. You have seconds to choose how hard you push the repair — and how much you're willing to risk.

Before any dice are rolled, choose one of six color-coded Tiers, from Green (safest) to Red (the Jackpot). Your Tier sets a ceiling on the Champion Die — the highest value it may show and still count. To win a roll, the Champion Die must beat or match the Opposing Die and land at or under your chosen ceiling. Lower Tiers are far less likely to hit, but pay out much bigger on a win. You commit to your Tier before rolling and can't change it once the dice are in hand.

Pull the Fuse

Puzzle Solving

2× D6 — Red & Blue 3 Rolls (Pulses)

Sparks cascade across the junction box. Six fuses, all live, and the whole grid threatening to blow. You have three chances to shut them down before the surge tears through the bulkhead.

Six fuses, numbered 1 through 6, need to be shut within 3 Pulses. Every roll can shut fuses in three ways at once: by each die's individual face value, by the sum of both dice (only valid if 6 or lower), and by the difference between them. A roll of 2 and 5, for example, offers fuses [2], [5], and [3] — the sum of 7 is invalid since fuses only go to 6.

System Surge — Doubles

Rolling doubles shuts the matching fuse as normal, then lets you pick one additional open fuse of your choice to shut on top of it.

OutcomeConditionConsequence
WINAll 6 fuses shut within 3 PulsesWagered resource returned plus one additional resource of the same type.
FAILAny fuse remains open after the 3rd PulseWagered resource lost. +1 Noise for each fuse still open.

Dead Reckoning

Coordinate Bet

2× D6 — Blue (Row) & Red (Column) 1 Roll

The targeting system flickers. A grid of coordinates scrolls across the display. You have one shot to lock in the right position before the window closes.

A 3×3 grid is formed from Blue Die ranges (rows, bottom to top: 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6) and Red Die ranges (columns, left to right: 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6). Place your wager token on one cell before rolling — this can't be changed once placed. Both dice are rolled together: the Blue Die picks the row, the Red Die picks the column.

OutcomeConditionConsequence
Direct HitRolled coordinate matches your chosen cell exactlyWagered resource returned plus one additional resource of the same type.
Partial HitRolled coordinate matches the row OR the column, not bothWagered resource returned. No bonus.
MissRolled coordinate matches neither the row nor the columnWagered resource lost. +2 Noise added to the Noise Tracker.

Cascade Failure

Push Your Luck

2× D6 rolled together 3 Rolls (Pulses)

The reactor coolant is venting. Warning indicators trip one after another across the panel in a chain you can barely keep up with. Every second you spend stabilizing makes the next problem worse. At some point, you have to decide: enough, or one more push?

Roll both dice and add the sum to a running total, up to 3 Pulses. After each Pulse you may voluntarily stop, or keep pushing. You must declare your intent to stop before rolling the next Pulse — no retroactive partial wins.

OutcomeConditionConsequence
Full WinRunning total lands at 18–2118–20 doubles the wager; exactly 21 triples it.
Partial WinRunning total is 14–17 and you voluntarily stopWagered resource returned. No bonus.
OverloadRunning total passes 21Automatic failure regardless of remaining Pulses.
Fail3 Pulses exhausted below 14, or Overload triggeredWagered resource lost. +3 Noise, plus +1 Noise for every 1 rolled on any die, any Pulse — win or lose.

Dead Signal

Sequential Match

2× D6 rolled together 3 Rolls (Pulses)

Static. Then something beneath it — a pattern, a rhythm, almost like a voice. The frequency is fragmenting. You lock in the coordinates and try to hold the signal long enough to confirm it. Whatever is transmitting out there, it knows you're listening.

An initial free roll establishes two Signal Values — one per die, and they can be a duplicate pair, like [3, 3]. These are locked for the mini-game. You then get up to 3 Pulses to roll both dice and match them against the remaining unconfirmed Signal Values. If a die matches an unconfirmed value, that value is confirmed and stays confirmed — progress carries between Pulses. A duplicate signal like [3, 3] needs two separate 3s to fully confirm, whether that's both in one Pulse or one at a time across two.

OutcomeConditionConsequence
WINBoth Signal Values confirmed within 3 PulsesWagered resource returned plus one additional resource of the same type, plus a Data Token placed at any discovered terminal or data point.
FAIL3 Pulses exhausted with one or both values unconfirmedWagered resource lost. +2 Noise per unconfirmed value (max +4). The creature advances +1 space toward the active player.
▶ Play Dead Signal
