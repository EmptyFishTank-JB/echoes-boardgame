Call a coordinate on the Red/Blue grid, then roll once. Land it exactly and your wager doubles — miss and the creature notices.

Win: Roll matches your called coordinate exactly — wager returned, doubled.
Near Miss: Exactly one die matches your call — wager lost, +2 Noise, Creature +1.
Miss: Neither die matches — wager lost, +4 Noise, Creature +1.
