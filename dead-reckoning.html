Dead Reckoning

Call a zone on the Red/Blue grid, then roll once. Land your zone exactly and your wager doubles — miss it and the creature notices.

Wager: Stake one resource before rolling. No resource = automatic failure.
Exact Win: Both zones match your call — wager returned, doubled.
Zone Win: Only the Red or only the Blue zone matches — wager returned.
Miss: Neither zone matches — wager lost, +2 Noise.
