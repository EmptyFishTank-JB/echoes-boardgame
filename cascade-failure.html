// Echo Event 04 — Push Your Luck

Cascade Failure

Roll up to three times, stacking a running total across every die. Bank between 18 and 21 and your wager doubles — overshoot, or come up short, and the reactor spikes.

Wager: Stake one resource before rolling. No resource = automatic failure.
Win: Bank a running total of 18–21 — wager returned, doubled.
Bust: Total exceeds 21 — wager lost, +3 Noise + 1 per “1” rolled.
Short: Bank (or run out of rolls) under 18 — wager lost, +3 Noise + 1 per “1” rolled.
