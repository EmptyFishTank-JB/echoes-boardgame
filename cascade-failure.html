// Echo Event 04 — Push Your Luck

Cascade Failure

Roll up to three times, stacking a running total across every die. Bank between 18 and 21 and your wager doubles — overshoot, or come up short, and the reactor spikes.

Wager: Stake one resource before rolling. No resource = automatic failure.
Win: Bank 18–20 — wager doubled. Bank exactly 21 — wager tripled.
Partial Win: Bank 14–17 — wager returned, no bonus.
Bust: Total exceeds 21 — wager lost, +3 Noise.
Short: Run out of rolls under 14 — wager lost, +3 Noise.
Every Run: +1 Noise per “1” rolled, whether you win or lose.
